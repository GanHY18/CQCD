GETTING AROUND

Transport plan

Chongqing

Use the metro for longer city journeys. For steep streets, awkward station exits or tired children, split into smaller ride-hailing groups.

Chongqing → Chengdu

High-speed rail between the cities. Choose a service that gives the group time to reach the station, collect everyone and board together.

Jiuzhaigou day trip · 11 March

High-speed rail from Chengdu East (成都东站) to Huanglongjiuzhai (黄龙九寨站), then a pre-booked private vehicle to the park and back. Use the park's sightseeing buses inside the scenic area.

Chengdu → TFU airport

Allow a generous airport transfer buffer for 14 people and luggage. Final route depends on the Chengdu hotel location and the day's metro timetable.

GROUP TIP

Keep 14 people moving smoothly

  • Nominate one group leader and one backup.
  • Share station exits and meeting points in the group chat.
  • Count everyone before leaving each station.
  • Keep the toddler's stroller foldable.
  • Carry snacks, water and warm layers for the mountain day.
